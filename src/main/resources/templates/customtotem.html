Emergency Totems

Emergency Totems are compact safety markers designed to fit inside your CamelBak and stay ready for hikes, festivals, trips, and long days outdoors. Each totem can be equipped with a customizable RFID tag for socials, contact info, or a personal link.

Trail-ready form

Slim enough to pack into a CamelBak pocket while still being easy to grab when you need it.

Custom RFID sharing

Add a programmable RFID tag that can point to socials, a Linktree-style profile, emergency contact details, or another personal URL.

Made for connection

A lightweight conversation piece with a real utility layer for safety, identification, and quick sharing.
