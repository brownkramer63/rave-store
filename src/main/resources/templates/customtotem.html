Emergency Totem

Emergency Totems are compact safety markers designed to fit inside your CamelBak and stay ready for hikes, festivals, trips, and long days outdoors. Each totem can be equipped with a customizable RFID tag, giving you a quick, fun way to share socials, contact info, or a personal link with the people you meet.

Trail-ready form

Slim enough to pack into a CamelBak pocket while still being easy to grab when you need it. Built for practical carry instead of sitting on a shelf.

Custom RFID sharing

Add a programmable RFID tag that can point to your socials, a Linktree-style profile, emergency contact details, or another personal URL.

Made for connection

A lightweight conversation piece with a real utility layer: useful for safety, identification, and sharing your world without awkwardly spelling out usernames.
